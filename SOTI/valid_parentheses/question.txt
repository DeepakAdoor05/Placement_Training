Given a string str consisting of opening and closing parentheses '(' and ')', 
the task is to find the length of the longest valid parenthesis substring.

Examples:
    input: str = "((()"
    output: 2
    explanation: Longest valid parentheses substring is "()".

    input: str = ")()())"
    output: 4
    explanation: Longest valid parentheses substring is "()()".
