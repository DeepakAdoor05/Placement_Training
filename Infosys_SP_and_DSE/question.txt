Wael is well-known for how much he loves the bit-wise operation, while kaito is well-known for how much he loves to sum numbers,
so their friend Resli decided to make up a problem that would enjoy both of them. Resli wrote down an array A of length N, an 
integer K and he defined a new function called Xor-sum as follows.

Xor-sum(x) = (xXOR A[1])+(xXOR A[1])+(xXOR A[1])+.......+(xXOR A[1])

Can you finnd the integer x in the range [0,k] with the maximum Xor-sum (x) value?
print only the value.

Sample Input:
n = 3
k = 7
arr = [1,6,3]

Sample Output:
14
